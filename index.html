Hello, I'm Abdelaziz Khadra

I am a 21-year-old student from Gaza who loves turning ideas into useful mobile applications. I design clean, simple interfaces and then bring them to life with code. My main focus is Android development using Java, and I am growing my skills in UI/UX design. I believe good software should solve real problems for real people, even in difficult conditions.

Current status: Second-year university student in Mobile Application Design and Programming, open to internships and freelance projects.

About Me

My Background

I grew up in Gaza and started university to study mobile application design and programming. So far I have completed about one-third of my study plan, with courses in Java, Android development, SQLite, and database systems. Studying and building projects under hard circumstances has taught me patience, focus, and how to make the most of limited resources.

My Interests

Besides programming, I am interested in UI/UX design, photography, and storytelling. I enjoy sharing stories about life in Gaza through content and photos, and I like designing brand identities and app interfaces in Figma.

Career Objectives

My goal is to become a professional Android developer with strong expertise in integrating artificial intelligence into mobile apps. I want to build products that are both beautiful to use and technically solid, and to work with teams that create real impact.
